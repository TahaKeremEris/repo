BridgeFix

Version 1.2 · iOS 15 · rootless

Stops the iOS 15 Watch app from crashing during Apple Watch setup when you pair a watch on a newer watchOS (e.g. watchOS 11) with a pairing tweak such as Legizmo.

Symptom it fixes: after pairing, tapping Continue closes the Watch app, and reopening it shows "Apple Watch is being unpaired". The crash log mentions BPSRemoteUISetupStyle applyToObjectModel:.

Some setup pages are sent by Apple's servers. The one that breaks is the online Terms and Conditions page shown right after activation. It now contains a modern web view (WKWebView), but iOS 15's styling code still calls an old UIWebView method on it (stringByEvaluatingJavaScriptFromString:), and the app crashes. BridgeFix catches that error so setup can continue. The Terms page may look slightly unstyled.

BridgeFix does not unlock pairing by itself. You still need a pairing tweak.

Tested on

JailbreakDopamine (rootless)
WatchSeries 10, watchOS 11.5
